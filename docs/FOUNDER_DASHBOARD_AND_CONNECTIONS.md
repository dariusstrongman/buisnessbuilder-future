# Founder Dashboard and Connections — canonical v1

This document locks the permanent product boundary. Build Room is created when a founder signs up and remains the same company workspace after the build. When Verification says the business is Ready, the interface calls it the Business Dashboard; Fully Set remains a separate Verification result. Take the Keys and Build & Run are modes of the same company dashboard, not migrations to another product. Build Room is a projection, never execution or readiness truth.

## Authorities

- Identity provides server-derived founder, tenant, company, membership and operator scope. A browser cannot set its own role, connection health, company authority or provider account identity.
- Company Brain owns canonical business facts. It stores no credential values.
- Commercial owns Business Builder orders, subscriptions and entitlements. A customer's Stripe business payment account is **not** the same thing as Business Builder's Stripe Checkout billing relationship.
- Runtime authorizes work, permissions, approvals and per-company job budgets; the AI Workforce policy selects allowed roles/capabilities. No connection may authorize a job by itself.
- Provider Connection owns customer-owned external account lifecycle, refresh, reconciliation and normalized health. The Secret/Artifact Broker resolves exact opaque credential refs only for Runtime-approved jobs. Credentials are never returned in dashboard projections.
- Founder Actions are real founder-controlled steps, not automatic success badges. Reconnection, new scope approval, credits, billing or terms may create an action. A health improvement never silently marks a residential-cleaning Founder Action `VERIFIED`.
- Verification alone owns Ready and Fully Set. Connection health is evidence or a blocker, not an alternate readiness authority.

## Provider-neutral object and credential policy

Each durable `ProviderConnection` is tenant/company scoped and has a capability (`EMAIL`, `CALENDAR`, `CRM`, `PAYMENTS`, `SCHEDULING`, `ACCOUNTING`, `ANALYTICS`, `LOCAL_PRESENCE`, `DOMAIN_DNS`), provider, account type, safe account identity, requested/granted scopes, connection timestamps, auth method, ownership classification and opaque secret-ref IDs. Ownership defaults to `unverified`, never a fabricated business-owned claim; sandbox adapters can attest their synthetic dedicated accounts. Provider adapters operate beneath capabilities; they cannot invent an AI Workforce grant. `ExternalAccount`, `ExternalCredentialRef`, `CapabilityGrant`, and broker receipts remain the existing durable objects. No parallel credential store is allowed.

Prefer dedicated **customer-owned business accounts** (such as `ops@business.com`, `support@business.com`, `bookings@business.com`) and a dedicated business calendar. A personal founder account is a documented legacy exception, never the default. The founder authorizes scoped access; Business Builder does not take account ownership.

Credential preference: provider-native OAuth/delegated authorization, scoped provider token, API key, app-specific credential, then username/password only for an explicitly reviewed legacy fallback. Last-resort credentials still go through approved encrypted secret storage. Raw keys, OAuth refresh tokens and passwords never enter Company Brain, normal PostgreSQL domain columns, SQS jobs, prompts, audit payloads, logs, frontend bundles or responses. Jobs carry opaque refs only. Scoped-key admission has an authenticated idempotency command record containing only provider/safe account identity; conflicting reuse fails closed. Revocation and rotation keep safe history; disconnect revokes Business Builder access where the provider supports it, disables the credential ref, and never deletes customer-owned provider data.

Business Builder's AI/runtime uses **our** model-provider accounts and credentials by default, under Runtime's per-company reservation/settlement budget, health-aware provider-neutral model router and hard job ceilings. Customers do not need OpenAI, Anthropic or Bedrock keys or separate model billing for normal plans. Customer-owned business systems use **their** provider accounts and authorizations. Optional AI BYOK may be considered later for enterprise/compliance needs; it is not a default plan requirement. No customer-facing token allowance is defined here.

## Lifecycle and health

The existing durable statuses map to founder lifecycle: no record = `NOT_CONNECTED`; `PENDING_AUTHORIZATION` = `AUTHORIZING`; `ACTIVE` = `CONNECTED`; persisted health warning/degradation = `DEGRADED` or `ACTION_REQUIRED`; an OAuth restart = `RECONNECTED`; `DISCONNECTED`/`REVOKED`/`EXPIRED` fail closed. A disconnect request is a privileged audited operation, not a user-authored status mutation.

Durable health is one of `HEALTHY`, `WARNING`, `ACTION_REQUIRED`, `DISCONNECTED`, `AUTH_EXPIRED`, `PERMISSION_ERROR`, `RATE_LIMITED`, `BILLING_OR_CREDITS`, `PROVIDER_OUTAGE`, `UNKNOWN`. Store the last successful check, failure time, safe normalized error class, founder action kind, quota signal only when measured, and audit. `UNKNOWN` is not healthy. Normal founder messages are plain English; raw provider codes and payloads remain operator-only. A low-credits warning is emitted only from measured balance, provider quota/usage API, rate-limit headers or classified billing signals; never invent a balance. Persistent provider faults must not cause a worker hammer loop.

Connect/reconnect use founder-authenticated OAuth state/PKCE/session binding or a scoped secret-ref registration flow. Founder reconnection may require fresh OAuth scopes. An adapter health signal requesting founder intervention appends a durable Company Brain Founder Action with `prepared` state and required provider-result evidence. Duplicate signals and later healthy checks never silently advance it; the existing residential-cleaning evidence/review/Verification pathway is not silently repurposed for connection actions. Disconnect revokes/rotates broker access; it cannot delete remote records. SUPPORT does not gain founder authority or silently override connection approval. A Runtime worker rechecks active entitlement, job scope, grants, connection status and health immediately before a provider-backed action; `UNKNOWN`, expired, revoked, rate-limited, billing-blocked and outage state deny or safely defer execution. The broker retains receipt/idempotency protections.

## Permanent dashboard projection

`GET /api/v1/companies/{company_id}/build-room` remains the dashboard projection. It contains Company Brain company status, Runtime work/approvals/budget, Commercial orders/subscriptions/entitlements, real Founder Actions, evidence/Verification, handoff and credential-free Connections. `GET /api/v1/companies/{company_id}/connections` is a narrow Connections projection; existing provider-connection endpoints own lifecycle. A connection card shows provider/capability, safe connected identity, scopes, last healthy check, normalized health, action needed, reconnect/disconnect affordances. Providers not configured are not shown as connected. Website, CRM, scheduling, payments, leads, documents, assets, support and recent agent actions appear only when the underlying authorities actually have records; empty/unavailable is honest, not simulated progress. Runtime budget sums include only budgets actually referenced by agent-runtime jobs, not zero-spend Founder Action budgets or a fabricated customer token quota. Runtime's existing shared company budget and per-job ceilings are the hard caps; its model router chooses the lowest-cost candidate only after security, capability, health, quality and quota eligibility.

## v1 proof and remaining live-provider boundaries

The current OAuth/PKCE sandbox email adapter and production-shaped OAuth port prove delegated authorization, token reference storage, refresh, revocation and callback authenticity. A test calendar OAuth adapter can prove the `CALENDAR` capability, but no Google application is registered or granted real Workspace access in v1. Existing Stripe Checkout is our commercial billing relationship and can be projected in billing; it is **not** a customer-owned `PAYMENTS` Connection. A mock API-key adapter proves brokered secret refs and scope without live secrets. Google Calendar/Workspace, Stripe Connect for customer-owned payments, actual CRM/accounting/domain adapters, provider revocation APIs, full quota APIs and real account ownership checks remain integration work. This branch does not enable any live external action or deploy infrastructure.

## Production review before live Connections

Provider app registration and scope review; a real callback and refresh/revocation test; exact secret-store IAM policy; proof of business-owned account identity; health/quotas from measured APIs; operator monitoring and action ownership; signed callback verifier; verified scanner/privacy/retention readiness for attachments; and live worker retries/reconciliation must all be approved. A healthy Connections card cannot by itself imply Ready, Fully Set, lawful operation or customer-owned asset export completeness.
